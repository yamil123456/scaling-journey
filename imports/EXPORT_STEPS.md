## A) Claude export (do this first)

1. Open **https://claude.ai** and sign in.
2. Tap your **profile picture** (bottom left on computer, or menu on phone).
3. Open **Settings**.
4. Open **Privacy** (sometimes under Account / Data).
5. Tap **Export data** / **Export conversations**.
6. Confirm. Claude emails you when the zip is ready (can take minutes to hours).
7. Open that email → **Download** → save the zip.
8. Put the zip in `imports/raw/claude/` (or message me here with the file).

---

## B) ChatGPT export (includes Dot)

You said you don’t know how — here it is:

1. Open **https://chatgpt.com** and sign in (same account as Dot).
2. Tap your **profile picture** (bottom left).
3. Tap **Settings**.
4. Tap **Data controls**.
5. Tap **Export data** → confirm.
6. Wait for the email from OpenAI (“Your data export is ready”).
7. Open the email → **Download data export** → save the zip.
8. Put the zip in `imports/raw/chatgpt/`.

**Faster for Dot only:** open Dot in ChatGPT → copy a useful chat → paste into a Notes app → save as text → put in `imports/raw/chatgpt-dot/`.

## C) Gemini / Google Takeout (Quickest Exits work)

1. Open **https://takeout.google.com** on the Google account that has that work.
2. Tap **Deselect all**.
3. Turn on **Gemini** and/or **My Activity** (and Drive if your old Quickest files are there).
4. Tap **Next** → choose email delivery → **Create export**.
5. When Google emails you, download → put in `imports/raw/gemini/`.
